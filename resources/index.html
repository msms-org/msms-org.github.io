Governing documents

The Constitution sets out what the Society is and who governs it; the others set out how it operates day to day.

  • Constitution The Society's foundational document — purpose, membership categories, governing bodies, amendment procedures.
  • Bylaws Operational detail — how meetings run, what each officer does, financial thresholds, disciplinary procedures.
  • Code of Conduct Standards of behaviour expected of all members and officers.
