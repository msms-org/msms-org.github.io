Maliyadeva Simulation & Modelling Society

Join the society

Any student enrolled at Maliyadeva College can apply. There's no prior coding experience required and no minimum skill level — you start as an Associate Member and progress to Regular Membership through the work you do.

Open the application form

What you're joining

There are two kinds of membership. Everyone starts at the first one.

Associate Member

Open to every enrolled student. Full access to workshops, projects, competitions, and Society resources. No voting rights, no eligibility for the Executive Committee — but no obligations either. You can come to as much or as little as you like while you work out whether it's for you.

Regular Member

Earned through active participation, by either contributing to a Society project or competing as part of an official team. A Director verifies the work and the Secretary records the change. Regular Members get voting rights and can stand for the Executive Committee.

What happens after you apply

Your application goes to the Secretary, who records you in the membership register. There's no selection process, no interview, and no waiting period — once you're recorded, you're a member.

You'll be told when the next session is and can turn up. Bring a laptop if you have one — we have spares if you don't. That's the whole process.
