Maliyadeva Simulation & Modelling Society

Join the society

Any student enrolled at Maliyadeva College can apply. There's no prior coding experience required and no minimum skill level.

Open the application form

What you're joining

There are two kinds of membership. Everyone starts at the first one.

Associate Member

Open to every enrolled student. Full access to workshops, projects, competitions, and Society resources. You can come to as much or as little as you like while you work out whether it's for you.

Regular Member

Earned through active participation, by either contributing to a Society project or competing as part of an official team. Regular Members get voting rights and can stand for the Executive Committee.

What happens after you apply

Your application goes to the Secretary, who records you in the membership register. There's no selection process, no interview, and no waiting period — once you're recorded, you're a member.

You'll be told when the next session is and can turn up.

Still not sure?

Email maliyadeva.sms@gmail.com and ask. Or just turn up to a session and see what it's like before you commit to anything.
