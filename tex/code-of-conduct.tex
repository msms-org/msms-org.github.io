\section*{Section 1. Purpose, Scope and Governing Principles}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 1. Purpose, Scope and Governing Principles}

\paragraph{1.1 Purpose}

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of conduct expected of
members and officers of the Maliyadeva Simulation and Modelling Society
(MSMS). It is intended to maintain a respectful, safe, scientifically
rigorous, and collaborative environment in which members can learn,
work, and contribute effectively.

\paragraph{1.2 Application}

This Code applies to all members and officers of the Society and, where
applicable, to any person participating in a Society activity, project,
competition, or other undertaking.

It applies to conduct occurring in connection with the Society,
including at Society meetings, events, projects, competitions,
programmes, external engagements, and on Society accounts, platforms,
repositories, or other digital resources.

\paragraph{1.3 Relationship with College Rules and Applicable Law}

This Code does not replace or supersede the rules of Maliyadeva College
or applicable law. Members remain subject to College requirements at
all times, and nothing in this Code limits the authority or disciplinary
jurisdiction of the College.

Where a matter is governed by College requirements, the applicable
College rules and procedures shall prevail.

\paragraph{1.4 Governing Principles}

Members and officers shall conduct themselves in accordance with the
following principles:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Respect and dignity.} Treat others with respect and
    dignity.
  \item \textbf{Integrity and honesty.} Act honestly and maintain
    academic and technical integrity.
  \item \textbf{Responsibility.} Fulfil commitments, take responsibility
    for one's conduct, and use reasonable care in Society activities.
  \item \textbf{Fairness.} Treat others fairly and without unreasonable
    discrimination.
  \item \textbf{Safety.} Act with reasonable care for the safety and
    well-being of oneself and others.
  \item \textbf{Responsible use of resources.} Use Society property,
    funds, information, and digital resources responsibly and for their
    intended purposes.
  \item \textbf{Accountability.} Comply with applicable Society
    procedures, decisions, and College requirements.
\end{enumerate}

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 2. Expected Conduct}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 2. Expected Conduct}

Members and officers are expected to conduct themselves responsibly in
all matters connected with the Society. In particular, they shall:

\paragraph{2.1 Respectful Conduct}

Treat other members, officers, teachers, guests, partners, and
participants with respect and dignity, and communicate in a
constructive and professional manner.

\paragraph{2.2 Responsible Participation}

Participate responsibly in Society activities, projects, competitions,
and meetings; fulfil commitments they undertake; follow applicable
instructions and procedures; comply with approved project or
competition requirements and applicable technical standards; obtain
approval for material changes where required; and contribute to a
cooperative working environment.

\paragraph{2.3 Safety and Proper Use of Facilities}

Comply with applicable safety requirements and use College premises,
Society facilities, equipment, and other resources with reasonable care
and for their intended purposes.

\paragraph{2.4 Responsible Digital Conduct}

Use Society accounts, repositories, communication platforms, shared
documents, and other digital resources responsibly; protect
credentials and access; and promptly report security concerns,
suspected breaches, or loss of access to the appropriate officer.

\paragraph{2.5 Academic and Technical Integrity}

Carry out Society work honestly and responsibly; accurately represent
data, results, code, sources, and contributions; submit work that the
member can explain and defend; and disclose the use of AI tools where
required by the Society, a project, or a competition.

\paragraph{2.6 Responsible Representation}

Members authorised to represent the Society shall communicate
accurately, respectfully, and within the scope of their authority, and
shall report relevant external communications as required by the
Bylaws.

\section*{Section 3. Prohibited Conduct}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 3. Prohibited Conduct}

The conduct described in this Section is prohibited. A breach may be
addressed under the disciplinary procedures of the Bylaws and, where
applicable, referred to the College.

\paragraph{3.1 Conduct Reserved to the College}

Conduct that is subject to disciplinary regulation by the College
shall remain governed by the applicable College requirements. The
Society shall not establish, apply, or maintain a separate or
inconsistent standard for matters reserved to the College.

Where conduct falling within the College's disciplinary authority
comes to the attention of the Executive Committee, the matter shall be
referred to the appropriate College authority in accordance with the
Bylaws, and the Society shall cooperate with any College inquiry or
procedure.

Nothing in this Section limits the authority of the Society to take
action within its own authority under the Bylaws, or to take
reasonable interim protective measures to protect members, Society
property, or the proper conduct of Society activities pending the
outcome of a College procedure. Such interim measures shall not
constitute a disciplinary sanction unless authorised as such under the
Bylaws.

\paragraph{3.2 Dishonesty and Misconduct}

Members shall not:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item engage in dishonesty, fraud, or deception in any Society
    matter;
  \item present the work of another person as their own, or present
    AI-generated work as their own without disclosure as required by
    Section 2.5;
  \item fabricate, falsify, or misrepresent data, results, code, or
    documentation; or
  \item engage in academic, technical, or competition misconduct.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{3.3 Misuse of Society Property, Funds, or Resources}

Members shall not:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item use Society funds, property, or resources for personal
    benefit or private purposes;
  \item remove, transfer, lend, dispose of, or damage Society property
    without authorization;
  \item misuse the Society's name, logo, identity, or accounts; or
  \item commit, conceal, or facilitate any financial irregularity
    prohibited by the Bylaws or the Financial Regulations.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{3.4 Unauthorised Representation and Misuse of Authority}

Members shall not:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item represent the Society externally, or hold themselves out as
    acting for the Society, without authorization;
  \item make statements or commitments on behalf of the Society that
    have not been authorised;
  \item exercise authority delegated to them beyond the scope and
    limits of that delegation; or
  \item purport to bind the Society, the College, or any affiliated
    body without authority.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{3.5 Misuse of Confidential Information, Data, or Digital Resources}

Members shall not:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item access, use, disclose, or retain confidential information,
    personal data, or Society records without authorization;
  \item share credentials, access tokens, or account access with any
    person not authorized to hold them;
  \item alter, destroy, conceal, or remove Society data, records, or
    digital assets without authorization; or
  \item use Society digital resources in a manner that risks the
    security, integrity, or continuity of the Society's systems.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{3.6 Serious Disruption}

Members shall not seriously disrupt, obstruct, or interfere with
Society activities, meetings, projects, or procedures.

\paragraph{3.7 Interference with Complaints and Investigations}

Members shall not interfere with, obstruct, or improperly influence a
complaint, investigation, or disciplinary proceeding under the Bylaws.

\paragraph{3.8 General Standard}

A member shall not intentionally or recklessly engage in conduct that,
in a serious and material manner, undermines the purposes, safety,
integrity, or lawful operation of the Society where the conduct is not
otherwise addressed by this Section. Ordinary disagreement, good-faith
criticism, or inadvertent error shall not, by itself, constitute a
breach of this provision.

\section*{Section 4. Reporting and Cooperation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 4. Reporting and Cooperation}

\paragraph{4.1 Reporting Concerns}

Concerns regarding alleged violations of this Code of Conduct shall be
reported in accordance with the disciplinary and complaint procedures
established by Article X of the Bylaws.

\paragraph{4.2 Good Faith}

Concerns should be raised in good faith. A concern that is later found
not to be established does not, by itself, constitute a breach of this
Code.

\paragraph{4.3 Cooperation}

Members shall cooperate honestly with any legitimate inquiry,
complaint, investigation, or disciplinary proceeding under the Bylaws.

\paragraph{4.4 No Retaliation}

No person shall retaliate against a person who raises a concern,
provides information, participates in an investigation, or otherwise
exercises a right under this Code or the Bylaws.

\paragraph{4.5 Confidentiality}

Information relating to a concern, complaint, or investigation shall
be treated confidentially to the extent reasonably practicable and
consistent with the Bylaws, College requirements, and applicable law.

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 5. Enforcement and Interpretation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 5. Enforcement and Interpretation}

Breaches of this Code shall be dealt with in accordance with the
disciplinary procedures established by the Bylaws.

Nothing in this Code limits the authority or disciplinary jurisdiction
of Maliyadeva College or the application of applicable law.

Where any provision of this Code conflicts with the Constitution, the
Bylaws, College requirements, or applicable law, the higher-ranking
instrument shall prevail, and the conflicting provision shall be void
to the extent of the conflict.
