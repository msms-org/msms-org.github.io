\section{Purpose and Scope}
% ==============================================================================

This Code of Conduct sets out how members and officers of the Maliyadeva
Simulation and Modelling Society (MSMS) are expected to behave. It exists
to keep the Society a respectful, safe, and honest place to learn and work.

It applies to all members and officers, and to anyone taking part in a
Society activity, project, competition, or event. It covers conduct at
meetings, events, projects, competitions, external activities, and on
Society accounts, platforms, and repositories.

This Code does not replace College rules or the law. Members remain
subject to Maliyadeva College requirements at all times, and College
rules and procedures take priority where they apply.

% ==============================================================================
\section{Expected Conduct}
% ==============================================================================

Members and officers are expected to:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Be respectful.} Treat members, officers, teachers, guests,
    partners, and participants with respect and dignity, and communicate
    constructively.
  \item \textbf{Be honest.} Act with integrity in all Society work, and
    represent data, results, code, sources, and contributions accurately.
  \item \textbf{Be responsible.} Fulfil commitments, follow instructions and
    approved project or competition requirements, and take reasonable care
    in Society activities.
  \item \textbf{Be fair.} Treat others fairly and without unreasonable
    discrimination.
  \item \textbf{Be safe.} Follow safety requirements and use College
    premises, Society equipment, and other resources with reasonable care.
  \item \textbf{Use resources properly.} Use Society funds, property,
    information, accounts, and digital resources only for their intended
    purposes.
  \item \textbf{Represent the Society properly.} If authorised to speak for
    the Society, do so accurately, respectfully, and only within the scope
    of that authority.
  \item \textbf{Protect access.} Keep Society credentials and accounts
    secure, and report lost access or security concerns to the appropriate
    officer promptly.
\end{enumerate}

% ==============================================================================
\section{Prohibited Conduct}
% ==============================================================================

The following conduct is prohibited. A breach may be dealt with under
Article X of the Bylaws and, where relevant, referred to the College.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dishonesty.} Fraud, deception, plagiarism, presenting
    AI-generated work as one's own without disclosure where required, or
    fabricating or falsifying data, results, code, or documentation.
  \item \textbf{Academic, technical, or competition misconduct.}
  \item \textbf{Misuse of Society property or funds.} Using Society funds,
    property, or resources for personal benefit; removing, lending,
    damaging, or disposing of Society property without permission; or any
    financial irregularity prohibited by the Bylaws.
  \item \textbf{Misuse of the Society's name or identity.} Using the
    Society's name, logo, identity, or accounts without authorisation, or
    implying endorsement or partnership without Executive Committee
    approval.
  \item \textbf{Unauthorised representation.} Speaking or committing on
    behalf of the Society without authority, or exceeding the authority
    delegated to an office.
  \item \textbf{Misuse of information or accounts.} Accessing, using,
    disclosing, or keeping confidential information, personal data, or
    Society records without authorisation; sharing passwords or account
    access with unauthorised persons; or deleting, altering, or removing
    Society data or records without permission.
  \item \textbf{Serious disruption.} Seriously disrupting or obstructing
    Society meetings, activities, projects, or procedures.
  \item \textbf{Interference or retaliation.} Interfering with a complaint
    or disciplinary matter, or retaliating against anyone who raises a
    concern or takes part in a process under this Code or the Bylaws.
  \item \textbf{Serious misconduct.} Any other conduct that seriously
    undermines the purposes, safety, or integrity of the Society.
    Ordinary disagreement, good-faith criticism, or honest mistake is not
    a breach of this item.
\end{enumerate}

Matters that fall under College disciplinary authority are handled by the
College. The Society shall refer such matters to the appropriate College
authority through the Teacher-in-Charge and shall cooperate with any
College process. Where necessary, the Society may take reasonable
protective measures while a College process is ongoing.

% ==============================================================================
\section{Reporting and Cooperation}
% ==============================================================================

Concerns about a possible breach of this Code shall be reported to the
Teacher-in-Charge (TIC) in accordance with Article X of the Bylaws.

Concerns should be raised in good faith. A concern that is not
established does not, by itself, breach this Code.

Members shall cooperate honestly with any legitimate inquiry or
disciplinary process under the Bylaws. Information about a concern,
complaint, or inquiry shall be kept confidential as far as is reasonably
practicable and consistent with the Bylaws, College requirements, and the
law.

No one shall retaliate against a person who raises a concern, provides
information, or takes part in such a process.

% ==============================================================================
\section{Enforcement}
% ==============================================================================

Breaches of this Code shall be handled under the disciplinary procedures
in Article X of the Bylaws.

Nothing in this Code limits the authority or disciplinary jurisdiction of
Maliyadeva College or the application of the law. Where this Code
conflicts with the Constitution, the Bylaws, College requirements, or the
law, the higher-ranking instrument prevails.
