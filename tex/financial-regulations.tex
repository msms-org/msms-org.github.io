\section*{Section 1. General Provisions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 1. General Provisions}

\subsubsection*{1.1 Purpose}
These Regulations prescribe the financial procedures and thresholds of
the Society under Article VIII of the Bylaws.

\subsubsection*{1.2 Financial Year}
The financial year of the Society shall run from 1 August to 31 July of
the following year.

\subsubsection*{1.3 Relationship with Other Instruments}
These Regulations operate under Article VIII of the Bylaws and shall be
interpreted in accordance with the Constitution and Bylaws.

Where these Regulations conflict with the Constitution, the Bylaws, or
applicable College requirements, the higher-ranking instrument shall
prevail.

Nothing in these Regulations limits the authority of Maliyadeva College.

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 2. Annual Budget}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 2. Annual Budget}

\subsubsection*{2.1 Preparation Timetable}
The annual budget shall be prepared according to the following
timetable relative to the Annual General Meeting (AGM):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Officers shall submit their anticipated financial requirements
    to the Treasurer at least thirty (30) days before the AGM.
    \item The Treasurer shall complete the draft annual budget at least
    twenty-one (21) days before the AGM.
    \item The Executive Committee shall approve the draft budget for
    submission to the General Assembly at least fourteen (14) days
    before the AGM.
    \item The approved draft budget shall be circulated to Regular
    Members at least seven (7) days before the AGM.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{2.2 Budget Changes}
Any amendment to the approved annual budget shall be made in accordance
with Article VIII, Section 4 of the Bylaws and any applicable approval
threshold under Section 3 of these Regulations.

\section*{Section 3. Financial Approval Thresholds}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 3. Financial Approval Thresholds}

\subsubsection*{3.1 Thresholds}
For the purposes of Article VIII, Section 4 of the Bylaws, the following
thresholds shall apply:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Routine Portfolio Expense:} Up to and including
    LKR 10,000

    \item \textbf{Executive Authorization:} More than LKR 10,000 and
    up to and including LKR 25,000

    \item \textbf{TIC Approval:} More than LKR 25,000

    \item \textbf{General Assembly Approval Threshold:} More than
    LKR 50,000, where General Assembly approval is required under
    Article VIII, Section 4(4) of the Bylaws.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{3.2 Application of Thresholds}
The thresholds shall be applied in accordance with the expenditure and
authorisation framework established by Article VIII, Section 4 of the
Bylaws.

Expenditures not provided for in the approved annual budget shall be
subject to the applicable approval and budget amendment requirements
under Article VIII, Section 4 of the Bylaws.

\subsubsection*{3.3 Transactions and Commitments}
The applicable threshold shall be determined by the total value of the
transaction, commitment, contract, or related expenditure. Related
transactions forming part of a single purchase, commitment, or
purpose shall be treated as one transaction for determining the
applicable threshold.

No person shall divide a transaction or commitment for the purpose of
avoiding an approval threshold.

The financial thresholds in this Section apply to monetary
expenditures, financial liabilities, and binding commitments. In-kind
sponsorships and donations shall be approved in accordance with
Article IX, Section 3 of the Bylaws.

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 4. Cash Handling and Deposit}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 4. Cash Handling and Deposit}

\subsubsection*{4.1 Receipting}
All cash received on behalf of the Society shall be acknowledged using
the official receipt method required by the Society and applicable
College requirements.

Each receipt shall be recorded in the Society's financial records,
including the date, amount, source, and purpose of the payment.

\subsubsection*{4.2 Deposit of Cash}
Cash received on behalf of the Society shall be deposited into the
designated Society account within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt or
by the next school working day, whichever requirement is later,
subject to applicable College requirements.

\section*{Section 5. Financial Irregularities}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 5. Financial Irregularities}

\subsubsection*{5.1 Definition}
A financial irregularity is any unauthorised, dishonest, deceptive, or
improper act or omission involving Society funds, property, financial
records, or other financial resources, including:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item unauthorised expenditure or use of Society funds or property;
    \item misuse, misappropriation, theft, or improper personal benefit
    involving Society funds or assets;
    \item submission of false, altered, or misleading claims, receipts,
    invoices, or reimbursement requests;
    \item falsification, alteration, destruction, or concealment of
    financial records or supporting documents;
    \item unauthorised withdrawals, transfers, borrowing, lending, or
    disposal of Society funds or property;
    \item splitting or restructuring transactions to evade an approval
    threshold or other financial control;
    \item failure to account for, return, or properly record Society
    funds entrusted to a person;
    \item unauthorised acceptance, use, transfer, or disposal of
    donations, sponsorships, grants, or other Society income; and
    \item knowingly assisting, concealing, or facilitating any of the
    foregoing.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{5.2 Reporting}
Suspected financial irregularities shall be reported in accordance with
the complaint and disciplinary procedures established under Article X
of the Bylaws and the Code of Conduct.

\section*{Section 6. Financial Records}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 6. Financial Records}

\subsubsection*{6.1 Financial Records}
The Treasurer shall maintain:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item an up-to-date physical cashbook for cash transactions; and
    \item a digital financial ledger recording the Society's financial
    transactions.
\end{enumerate}

Supporting financial documents shall be retained in accordance with the
Bylaws, these Regulations, and applicable College requirements.

\subsubsection*{6.2 Annual Financial Report}
The Annual Financial Report required under Article VIII, Section 5 of
the Bylaws shall provide a clear summary of the Society's financial
position and activities for the completed Financial Year.

The report shall include, at minimum:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Opening Balance:} The Society's financial balance at
    the beginning of the Financial Year.

    \item \textbf{Income:} A summary of all income received during the
    Financial Year.

    \item \textbf{Expenditure:} A summary of expenditure during the
    Financial Year, organised by portfolio and identifying material
    individual expenditures where appropriate.

    \item \textbf{Budget Comparison:} A comparison of the approved
    annual budget with actual income and expenditure.

    \item \textbf{Closing Balance:} The Society's financial balance at
    the end of the Financial Year, together with a reconciliation to
    the relevant financial records.

    \item \textbf{Commitments and Liabilities:} Any outstanding
    obligations, commitments, or liabilities of the Society at the end
    of the Financial Year.

    \item \textbf{Financial Irregularities:} Any financial
    irregularities identified during the Financial Year and the status
    of any resulting review or corrective action, subject to applicable
    confidentiality requirements.

    \item \textbf{Certification:} A statement by the Treasurer
    confirming that the report has been prepared from the Society's
    financial records and, to the best of the Treasurer's knowledge,
    presents a fair summary of the Society's financial position for
    the Financial Year.
\end{enumerate}

The Annual Financial Report shall be presented to the General Assembly
at the Annual General Meeting in accordance with Article VIII,
Section 5 of the Bylaws and shall be made available for inspection
subject to the governing documents of the Society and applicable
College requirements.
