\section*{Section 1. General Provisions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 1. General Provisions}

\subsection*{1.1 Purpose and Scope}
This Regulation prescribes the procedures for membership admission,
registration, progression, termination, and maintenance of the
Membership Register, and applies to applicants, members, the Executive
Committee, and the Secretary in the exercise of membership functions.

\subsection*{1.2 Definitions}
For this Regulation, unless the context otherwise requires:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Application Form} means the form in Schedule A.
    \item \textbf{Progression Form} means the form in Schedule B.
    \item \textbf{Register} means the Membership Register maintained by
    the Secretary.
    \item \textbf{Member ID} means the identifier assigned under
    Section 3.3.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{1.3 Relationship with Other Instruments}
This Regulation is subject to the Constitution, Bylaws, other Society
Regulations, and applicable College requirements. Where a conflict
arises, the higher-ranking instrument shall prevail.

\subsection*{1.4 Amendment of Schedules}
The Secretary may, with the approval of the Executive Committee, make
non-substantive changes to a Schedule, including clerical corrections
or formatting changes. Substantive changes shall be made in accordance
with Article XI, Section 3 of the Bylaws.

\section*{Section 2. Application and Admission}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 2. Application and Admission}

\subsection*{2.1 Application}

An eligible student who wishes to apply for Associate Membership shall
submit a completed Application Form to the Secretary in physical or
electronic form.

The Secretary shall acknowledge receipt and record the date of
submission.

\subsection*{2.2 Verification of Eligibility}
Upon receipt of an Application Form, the Secretary shall verify that
the applicant is currently enrolled at Maliyadeva College.

If eligibility cannot be verified, the Secretary shall return the
application to the applicant with a written explanation.

\subsection*{2.3 Approval}
Admission may be approved by the Secretary or any other officer authorised by the EC.

\subsection*{2.4 Effective Date of Admission}
Admission as an Associate Member shall take effect when the Secretary
records the new member in the Register.

The Secretary shall assign a Member ID under Section 3.3 at the
time of recording.

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 3. Membership Register}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 3. Membership Register}

\subsection*{3.1 Format}
The Register shall be maintained in digital form, and shall serve as the primary working record, while the
physical records shall be authoritative for applications and approvals.

\subsection*{3.2 Digital Register}
The digital Register shall contain one row per member, with the
following columns: Member ID, Membership status (Associate / Regular /
Honorary), Full name, Preferred name, Class, School admissions number,
Email, WhatsApp number, Date of application, Date of admission, Areas
of interest, Prior experience, GitHub username, Date progressed to
Regular Membership (if applicable), Date Honorary Membership conferred
(if applicable), Date of termination (if applicable), Reason for
termination (if applicable), Last updated, Updated by, and Notes.

\subsection*{3.3 Member IDs}
A Member ID shall have the form:

\begin{center}
\textbf{MSMS-YY-NNNN}
\end{center}

where:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{YY} is the two-digit year in which the person first joined the Society; and
  \item \textbf{NNNN} is a four-digit sequence number assigned in order of first admission.
\end{enumerate}

A single sequence
counter shall be maintained per year.

\subsection*{3.4 Status Changes}
When a member progresses from Associate to Regular Membership, their
Membership Status attribute shall be updated from Associate to Regular
in the Register.

When Honorary Membership is conferred on a member, their Membership
Status attribute shall be updated to Honorary, unless the Bylaws
expressly permit Honorary Membership to be held concurrently with
another membership category.

A member's permanent Member ID remains unchanged
during any status update.

\subsection*{3.5 Accuracy and Updates}
The Secretary shall keep the Register accurate and up to date. Members
shall notify the Secretary of changes to their contact or academic
details within a reasonable time. The Secretary shall record each
update with the date and the officer making the change.

\subsection*{3.6 Access}
The following persons may access the Register:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the Secretary;
  \item the Executive Committee;
  \item the Teacher-in-Charge;
  \item the College, on request;
  \item a member, in respect of their own entry, on request.
\end{enumerate}

No other person may access the Register without the approval of the
Executive Committee.

Access shall be subject to any applicable College requirements on
personal data.

\section*{Section 4. Progression to Regular Membership}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 4. Progression to Regular Membership}

\subsection*{4.1 Application for Progression}
An Associate Member who wishes to progress to Regular Membership shall
notify the Secretary. The notification may be made verbally or in
writing.

The Secretary shall record the date of the request and shall refer the
matter to the appropriate verifying officer under Section 4.2 or 4.3.

\subsection*{4.2 Project Contribution Pathway}
Where the applicant relies on the Project Contribution Pathway, the
Secretary shall refer the matter to the Director of Technical Affairs
and Projects.

The Director shall:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item review the applicant's contribution to a Society-approved project;
  \item verify the applicant's comprehension of the relevant code or model, with reference to the code itself; and
  \item complete and sign the Project Contribution section of the Progression Form.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{4.3 Competition Pathway}
Where the applicant relies on the Competition Pathway, the Secretary
shall refer the matter to the relevant Team Leader.

The Team Leader shall confirm that the applicant actively represented
the Society as an official team member or contestant in a competition
approved by the Executive Committee, and shall complete and sign the
Competition section of the Progression Form.

Where there is no Team Leader, or where the Team Leader is the
applicant, the Director of Competitions and Events shall verify
participation in place of the Team Leader.

\subsection*{4.4 Recording of Progression}
Upon receipt of a completed Progression Form, the Secretary shall record the progression in the Register and update the member's status from Associate to Regular.

Progression takes effect when the Secretary records it.

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 5. Honorary Membership}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 5. Honorary Membership}

\subsection*{5.1 Nomination}
A member of the Executive Committee may nominate an individual for
Honorary Membership.

\subsection*{5.2 Consideration}

Honorary Membership is conferred by resolution of the Executive
Committee, in accordance with the Bylaws.

\subsection*{5.3 Recording}
When Honorary Membership is conferred, the Secretary shall:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item record the conferral in the Register; and
  \item assign a Member ID.
\end{enumerate}

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 6. Termination}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 6. Termination}

\subsection*{6.1 Voluntary Resignation}
A member may resign by submitting written notice to the Secretary. Any
signed and dated written notice is sufficient; no specific form is
required.

The Secretary shall record the resignation and the effective date in
the Register, and shall file the notice in the physical Register.

\subsection*{6.2 Loss of Eligibility}
Membership ceases where a member no longer satisfies the eligibility
requirements applicable to their category.

The Secretary shall determine loss of eligibility at the start of each
academic term by reference to the College register, and shall record
the cessation in the Register.

\subsection*{6.3 Disciplinary Removal}
Membership may be terminated as a disciplinary sanction under Article X
of the Bylaws.

Where termination is imposed, the Secretary shall record the cessation
and shall file the disciplinary decision in the physical Register.

\subsection*{6.4 Termination Record}
For every cessation of membership, the Secretary shall complete a
Termination Record stating:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the Member ID and name of the member;
  \item the ground for cessation;
  \item the effective date; and
  \item the officer recording the cessation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{6.5 Re-application}
A person whose membership ceased may re-apply as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{After voluntary resignation} --- the person may re-apply as a new applicant, with no Executive Committee approval required beyond the ordinary admission procedure.
  \item \textbf{After loss of eligibility} --- the person may re-apply once they again satisfy the eligibility requirements applicable to their category, following the ordinary admission procedure.
  \item \textbf{After disciplinary removal} --- the person may re-apply only with the prior approval of the Executive Committee.
\end{enumerate}

Where the original record still exists, the Secretary shall reinstate
the original Member ID and update their status as necessary. Where
the original record no longer exists, a new Member ID shall be
assigned.

% ==============================================================================
\section*{Section 7. Records and Interpretation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Section 7. Records and Interpretation}

\subsection*{7.1 Custody and Retention}
The Register and all supporting forms shall be maintained by the
Secretary as part of the official records of the Society.

Records shall be retained for the period prescribed by the Bylaws, the
Financial Regulations, or applicable College requirements, whichever
is longer.

\subsection*{7.2 Handover}

Upon a change of Secretary, the outgoing Secretary shall transfer the
Register, the physical binder, and all related access credentials to
the incoming Secretary in accordance with the applicable handover
requirements.

\subsection*{7.3 Interpretation and College Authority}
This Regulation shall be interpreted in accordance with Article XII of
the Bylaws. Nothing in this Regulation limits the authority of the
College.
