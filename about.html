Maliyadeva Simulation & Modelling Society

About the society

We're a student-led society at Maliyadeva College, Kurunegala, for anyone who likes taking a system apart, writing down the rules, and letting a computer tell us what happens next.

What we're here to do

The Society exists to advance computer simulation, computational modelling, and applied systems thinking among students at Maliyadeva College.

That covers more ground than it sounds like. It means building technical skill and academic understanding, running collaborative projects that hold up to review, representing the College in approved competitions, and making sure what one year's members learn doesn't disappear when they graduate.

What we do

Learning

Building interest, technical skill, and academic understanding in simulation, modelling, and applied systems thinking.

Projects

Collaborative, research-inspired work with reproducible technical standards.

Competitions

Representing the Society in approved external competitions and programmes.

Mentoring

Transferring knowledge between experienced and newer members, so skills stay in the Society.

Who can join

Any student currently enrolled at Maliyadeva College can apply. You start as an Associate Member, which is open to everyone and comes with access to workshops, projects, and competitions. No prior coding experience is required, and there is no minimum skill level.

Regular Membership — which carries voting rights and eligibility for the Executive Committee — is earned through active participation. There are two routes: contributing to a Society project, or competing as part of an official team. Either way, a Director verifies the work and the Secretary records the change.
