About the club

We're a student-run group for people who like taking a system apart, writing down the rules, and letting a computer tell us what happens next. Some of us come from physics, some from CS, some just from a stubborn curiosity about how things work.

What a meeting looks like

Usually: 15 minutes of announcements, a short walkthrough of a technique or paper, then an hour of building. We pair people up so nobody's stuck staring at an error message alone.

Tools we use

Python

NumPy, SciPy, SimPy, Mesa. Our default for almost everything.

NetLogo

Fastest way to get an agent-based model on screen.

C++

When the simulation needs to actually finish today.

Who can join

Any student at the school. There's no application and no minimum skill level — we've had members who'd never written code before and members who'd been doing it for years. Sign up here.
