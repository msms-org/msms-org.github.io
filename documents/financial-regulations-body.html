FINANCIAL REGULATIONS
Maliyadeva Simulation and Modelling Society (MSMS)
Maliyadeva College

Section 1. General Provisions

1.1 Purpose

These Regulations prescribe the financial procedures and thresholds of the Society under Article VIII of the Bylaws.

1.2 Financial Year

The financial year of the Society shall run from 1 August to 31 July of the following year.

1.3 Relationship with Other Instruments

These Regulations operate under Article VIII of the Bylaws and shall be interpreted in accordance with the Constitution and Bylaws.

Where these Regulations conflict with the Constitution, the Bylaws, or applicable College requirements, the higher-ranking instrument shall prevail.

Nothing in these Regulations limits the authority of Maliyadeva College.

Section 2. Annual Budget

2.1 Preparation Timetable

The annual budget shall be prepared according to the following timetable relative to the Annual General Meeting (AGM):

  1. Officers shall submit their anticipated financial requirements to the Treasurer at least thirty (30) days before the AGM.

  2. The Treasurer shall complete the draft annual budget at least twenty-one (21) days before the AGM.

  3. The Executive Committee shall approve the draft budget for submission to the General Assembly at least fourteen (14) days before the AGM.

  4. The approved draft budget shall be circulated to Regular Members at least seven (7) days before the AGM.

2.2 Budget Changes

Any amendment to the approved annual budget shall be made in accordance with Article VIII, Section 4 of the Bylaws and any applicable approval threshold under Section 3 of these Regulations.

Section 3. Financial Approval Thresholds

3.1 Thresholds

For the purposes of Article VIII, Section 4 of the Bylaws, the following thresholds shall apply:

  1. Routine Portfolio Expense: Up to and including LKR 10,000

  2. Executive Authorization: More than LKR 10,000 and up to and including LKR 25,000

  3. TIC Approval: More than LKR 25,000

  4. General Assembly Approval Threshold: More than LKR 50,000, where General Assembly approval is required under Article VIII, Section 4(4) of the Bylaws.

3.2 Application of Thresholds

The thresholds shall be applied in accordance with the expenditure and authorisation framework established by Article VIII, Section 4 of the Bylaws.

Expenditures not provided for in the approved annual budget shall be subject to the applicable approval and budget amendment requirements under Article VIII, Section 4 of the Bylaws.

3.3 Transactions and Commitments

The applicable threshold shall be determined by the total value of the transaction, commitment, contract, or related expenditure. Related transactions forming part of a single purchase, commitment, or purpose shall be treated as one transaction for determining the applicable threshold.

No person shall divide a transaction or commitment for the purpose of avoiding an approval threshold.

The financial thresholds in this Section apply to monetary expenditures, financial liabilities, and binding commitments. In-kind sponsorships and donations shall be approved in accordance with Article IX, Section 3 of the Bylaws.

Section 4. Cash Handling and Deposit

4.1 Receipting

All cash received on behalf of the Society shall be acknowledged using the official receipt method required by the Society and applicable College requirements.

Each receipt shall be recorded in the Society’s financial records, including the date, amount, source, and purpose of the payment.

4.2 Deposit of Cash

Cash received on behalf of the Society shall be deposited into the designated Society account within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt or by the next school working day, whichever requirement is later, subject to applicable College requirements.

Section 5. Financial Irregularities

5.1 Definition

A financial irregularity is any unauthorised, dishonest, deceptive, or improper act or omission involving Society funds, property, financial records, or other financial resources, including:

  1. unauthorised expenditure or use of Society funds or property;

  2. misuse, misappropriation, theft, or improper personal benefit involving Society funds or assets;

  3. submission of false, altered, or misleading claims, receipts, invoices, or reimbursement requests;

  4. falsification, alteration, destruction, or concealment of financial records or supporting documents;

  5. unauthorised withdrawals, transfers, borrowing, lending, or disposal of Society funds or property;

  6. splitting or restructuring transactions to evade an approval threshold or other financial control;

  7. failure to account for, return, or properly record Society funds entrusted to a person;

  8. unauthorised acceptance, use, transfer, or disposal of donations, sponsorships, grants, or other Society income; and

  9. knowingly assisting, concealing, or facilitating any of the foregoing.

5.2 Reporting

Suspected financial irregularities shall be reported in accordance with the complaint and disciplinary procedures established under Article X of the Bylaws and the Code of Conduct.

Section 6. Financial Records

6.1 Financial Records

The Treasurer shall maintain:

  1. an up-to-date physical cashbook for cash transactions; and

  2. a digital financial ledger recording the Society’s financial transactions.

Supporting financial documents shall be retained in accordance with the Bylaws, these Regulations, and applicable College requirements.

6.2 Annual Financial Report

The Annual Financial Report required under Article VIII, Section 5 of the Bylaws shall provide a clear summary of the Society’s financial position and activities for the completed Financial Year.

The report shall include, at minimum:

  1. Opening Balance: The Society’s financial balance at the beginning of the Financial Year.

  2. Income: A summary of all income received during the Financial Year.

  3. Expenditure: A summary of expenditure during the Financial Year, organised by portfolio and identifying material individual expenditures where appropriate.

  4. Budget Comparison: A comparison of the approved annual budget with actual income and expenditure.

  5. Closing Balance: The Society’s financial balance at the end of the Financial Year, together with a reconciliation to the relevant financial records.

  6. Commitments and Liabilities: Any outstanding obligations, commitments, or liabilities of the Society at the end of the Financial Year.

  7. Financial Irregularities: Any financial irregularities identified during the Financial Year and the status of any resulting review or corrective action, subject to applicable confidentiality requirements.

  8. Certification: A statement by the Treasurer confirming that the report has been prepared from the Society’s financial records and, to the best of the Treasurer’s knowledge, presents a fair summary of the Society’s financial position for the Financial Year.

The Annual Financial Report shall be presented to the General Assembly at the Annual General Meeting in accordance with Article VIII, Section 5 of the Bylaws and shall be made available for inspection subject to the governing documents of the Society and applicable College requirements.
