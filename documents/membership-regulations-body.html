MEMBERSHIP REGULATIONS
Maliyadeva Simulation and Modelling Society (MSMS)
Maliyadeva College

Section 1. General Provisions

1.1 Purpose and Scope

This Regulation prescribes the procedures for membership admission, registration, progression, termination, and maintenance of the Membership Register, and applies to applicants, members, the Executive Committee, and the Secretary in the exercise of membership functions.

1.2 Definitions

For this Regulation, unless the context otherwise requires:

  1. Application Form means the form in Schedule A.

  2. Progression Form means the form in Schedule B.

  3. Register means the Membership Register maintained by the Secretary.

  4. Member ID means the identifier assigned under Section 3.3.

1.3 Relationship with Other Instruments

This Regulation is subject to the Constitution, Bylaws, other Society Regulations, and applicable College requirements. Where a conflict arises, the higher-ranking instrument shall prevail.

1.4 Amendment of Schedules

The Secretary may, with the approval of the Executive Committee, make non-substantive changes to a Schedule, including clerical corrections or formatting changes. Substantive changes shall be made in accordance with Article XI, Section 3 of the Bylaws.

Section 2. Application and Admission

2.1 Application

An eligible student who wishes to apply for Associate Membership shall submit a completed Application Form to the Secretary in physical or electronic form.

The Secretary shall acknowledge receipt and record the date of submission.

2.2 Verification of Eligibility

Upon receipt of an Application Form, the Secretary shall verify that the applicant is currently enrolled at Maliyadeva College.

If eligibility cannot be verified, the Secretary shall return the application to the applicant with a written explanation.

2.3 Approval

Admission may be approved by the Secretary or any other officer authorised by the EC.

2.4 Effective Date of Admission

Admission as an Associate Member shall take effect when the Secretary records the new member in the Register.

The Secretary shall assign a Member ID under Section 3.3 at the time of recording.

Section 3. Membership Register

3.1 Format

The Register shall be maintained in digital form, and shall serve as the primary working record, while the physical records shall be authoritative for applications and approvals.

3.2 Digital Register

The digital Register shall contain one row per member, with the following columns: Member ID, Membership status (Associate / Regular / Honorary), Full name, Preferred name, Class, School admissions number, Email, WhatsApp number, Date of application, Date of admission, Areas of interest, Prior experience, GitHub username, Date progressed to Regular Membership (if applicable), Date Honorary Membership conferred (if applicable), Date of termination (if applicable), Reason for termination (if applicable), Last updated, Updated by, and Notes.

3.3 Member IDs

A Member ID shall have the form:

MSMS-YY-NNNN

where:

  1. YY is the two-digit year in which the person first joined the Society; and

  2. NNNN is a four-digit sequence number assigned in order of first admission.

A single sequence counter shall be maintained per year.

3.4 Status Changes

When a member progresses from Associate to Regular Membership, their Membership Status attribute shall be updated from Associate to Regular in the Register.

When Honorary Membership is conferred on a member, their Membership Status attribute shall be updated to Honorary, unless the Bylaws expressly permit Honorary Membership to be held concurrently with another membership category.

A member’s permanent Member ID remains unchanged during any status update.

3.5 Accuracy and Updates

The Secretary shall keep the Register accurate and up to date. Members shall notify the Secretary of changes to their contact or academic details within a reasonable time. The Secretary shall record each update with the date and the officer making the change.

3.6 Access

The following persons may access the Register:

  1. the Secretary;

  2. the Executive Committee;

  3. the Teacher-in-Charge;

  4. the College, on request;

  5. a member, in respect of their own entry, on request.

No other person may access the Register without the approval of the Executive Committee.

Access shall be subject to any applicable College requirements on personal data.

Section 4. Progression to Regular Membership

4.1 Application for Progression

An Associate Member who wishes to progress to Regular Membership shall notify the Secretary. The notification may be made verbally or in writing.

The Secretary shall record the date of the request and shall refer the matter to the appropriate verifying officer under Section 4.2 or 4.3.

4.2 Project Contribution Pathway

Where the applicant relies on the Project Contribution Pathway, the Secretary shall refer the matter to the Director of Technical Affairs and Projects.

The Director shall:

  1. review the applicant’s contribution to a Society-approved project;

  2. verify the applicant’s comprehension of the relevant code or model, with reference to the code itself; and

  3. complete and sign the Project Contribution section of the Progression Form.

4.3 Competition Pathway

Where the applicant relies on the Competition Pathway, the Secretary shall refer the matter to the relevant Team Leader.

The Team Leader shall confirm that the applicant actively represented the Society as an official team member or contestant in a competition approved by the Executive Committee, and shall complete and sign the Competition section of the Progression Form.

Where there is no Team Leader, or where the Team Leader is the applicant, the Director of Competitions and Events shall verify participation in place of the Team Leader.

4.4 Recording of Progression

Upon receipt of a completed Progression Form, the Secretary shall record the progression in the Register and update the member’s status from Associate to Regular.

Progression takes effect when the Secretary records it.

Section 5. Honorary Membership

5.1 Nomination

A member of the Executive Committee may nominate an individual for Honorary Membership.

5.2 Consideration

Honorary Membership is conferred by resolution of the Executive Committee, in accordance with the Bylaws.

5.3 Recording

When Honorary Membership is conferred, the Secretary shall:

  1. record the conferral in the Register; and

  2. assign a Member ID.

Section 6. Termination

6.1 Voluntary Resignation

A member may resign by submitting written notice to the Secretary. Any signed and dated written notice is sufficient; no specific form is required.

The Secretary shall record the resignation and the effective date in the Register, and shall file the notice in the physical Register.

6.2 Loss of Eligibility

Membership ceases where a member no longer satisfies the eligibility requirements applicable to their category.

The Secretary shall determine loss of eligibility at the start of each academic term by reference to the College register, and shall record the cessation in the Register.

6.3 Disciplinary Removal

Membership may be terminated as a disciplinary sanction under Article X of the Bylaws.

Where termination is imposed, the Secretary shall record the cessation and shall file the disciplinary decision in the physical Register.

6.4 Termination Record

For every cessation of membership, the Secretary shall complete a Termination Record stating:

  1. the Member ID and name of the member;

  2. the ground for cessation;

  3. the effective date; and

  4. the officer recording the cessation.

6.5 Re-application

A person whose membership ceased may re-apply as follows:

  1. After voluntary resignation — the person may re-apply as a new applicant, with no Executive Committee approval required beyond the ordinary admission procedure.

  2. After loss of eligibility — the person may re-apply once they again satisfy the eligibility requirements applicable to their category, following the ordinary admission procedure.

  3. After disciplinary removal — the person may re-apply only with the prior approval of the Executive Committee.

Where the original record still exists, the Secretary shall reinstate the original Member ID and update their status as necessary. Where the original record no longer exists, a new Member ID shall be assigned.

Section 7. Records and Interpretation

7.1 Custody and Retention

The Register and all supporting forms shall be maintained by the Secretary as part of the official records of the Society.

Records shall be retained for the period prescribed by the Bylaws, the Financial Regulations, or applicable College requirements, whichever is longer.

7.2 Handover

Upon a change of Secretary, the outgoing Secretary shall transfer the Register, the physical binder, and all related access credentials to the incoming Secretary in accordance with the applicable handover requirements.

7.3 Interpretation and College Authority

This Regulation shall be interpreted in accordance with Article XII of the Bylaws. Nothing in this Regulation limits the authority of the College.
