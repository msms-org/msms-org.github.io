CODE OF CONDUCT
Maliyadeva Simulation and Modelling Society (MSMS)
Maliyadeva College

Purpose and Scope

This Code of Conduct sets out how members and officers of the Maliyadeva Simulation and Modelling Society (MSMS) are expected to behave. It exists to keep the Society a respectful, safe, and honest place to learn and work.

It applies to all members and officers, and to anyone taking part in a Society activity, project, competition, or event. It covers conduct at meetings, events, projects, competitions, external activities, and on Society accounts, platforms, and repositories.

This Code does not replace College rules or the law. Members remain subject to Maliyadeva College requirements at all times, and College rules and procedures take priority where they apply.

Expected Conduct

Members and officers are expected to:

  1. Be respectful. Treat members, officers, teachers, guests, partners, and participants with respect and dignity, and communicate constructively.

  2. Be honest. Act with integrity in all Society work, and represent data, results, code, sources, and contributions accurately.

  3. Be responsible. Fulfil commitments, follow instructions and approved project or competition requirements, and take reasonable care in Society activities.

  4. Be fair. Treat others fairly and without unreasonable discrimination.

  5. Be safe. Follow safety requirements and use College premises, Society equipment, and other resources with reasonable care.

  6. Use resources properly. Use Society funds, property, information, accounts, and digital resources only for their intended purposes.

  7. Represent the Society properly. If authorised to speak for the Society, do so accurately, respectfully, and only within the scope of that authority.

  8. Protect access. Keep Society credentials and accounts secure, and report lost access or security concerns to the appropriate officer promptly.

Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is prohibited. A breach may be dealt with under Article X of the Bylaws and, where relevant, referred to the College.

  1. Dishonesty. Fraud, deception, plagiarism, presenting AI-generated work as one’s own without disclosure where required, or fabricating or falsifying data, results, code, or documentation.

  2. Academic, technical, or competition misconduct.

  3. Misuse of Society property or funds. Using Society funds, property, or resources for personal benefit; removing, lending, damaging, or disposing of Society property without permission; or any financial irregularity prohibited by the Bylaws.

  4. Misuse of the Society’s name or identity. Using the Society’s name, logo, identity, or accounts without authorisation, or implying endorsement or partnership without Executive Committee approval.

  5. Unauthorised representation. Speaking or committing on behalf of the Society without authority, or exceeding the authority delegated to an office.

  6. Misuse of information or accounts. Accessing, using, disclosing, or keeping confidential information, personal data, or Society records without authorisation; sharing passwords or account access with unauthorised persons; or deleting, altering, or removing Society data or records without permission.

  7. Serious disruption. Seriously disrupting or obstructing Society meetings, activities, projects, or procedures.

  8. Interference or retaliation. Interfering with a complaint or disciplinary matter, or retaliating against anyone who raises a concern or takes part in a process under this Code or the Bylaws.

  9. Serious misconduct. Any other conduct that seriously undermines the purposes, safety, or integrity of the Society. Ordinary disagreement, good-faith criticism, or honest mistake is not a breach of this item.

Matters that fall under College disciplinary authority are handled by the College. The Society shall refer such matters to the appropriate College authority through the Teacher-in-Charge and shall cooperate with any College process. Where necessary, the Society may take reasonable protective measures while a College process is ongoing.

Reporting and Cooperation

Concerns about a possible breach of this Code shall be reported to the Teacher-in-Charge (TIC) in accordance with Article X of the Bylaws.

Concerns should be raised in good faith. A concern that is not established does not, by itself, breach this Code.

Members shall cooperate honestly with any legitimate inquiry or disciplinary process under the Bylaws. Information about a concern, complaint, or inquiry shall be kept confidential as far as is reasonably practicable and consistent with the Bylaws, College requirements, and the law.

No one shall retaliate against a person who raises a concern, provides information, or takes part in such a process.

Enforcement

Breaches of this Code shall be handled under the disciplinary procedures in Article X of the Bylaws.

Nothing in this Code limits the authority or disciplinary jurisdiction of Maliyadeva College or the application of the law. Where this Code conflicts with the Constitution, the Bylaws, College requirements, or the law, the higher-ranking instrument prevails.
