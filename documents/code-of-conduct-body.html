CODE OF CONDUCT
Maliyadeva Simulation and Modelling Society (MSMS)
Maliyadeva College

Section 1. Purpose, Scope and Governing Principles

1.1 Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of conduct expected of members and officers of the Maliyadeva Simulation and Modelling Society (MSMS). It is intended to maintain a respectful, safe, scientifically rigorous, and collaborative environment in which members can learn, work, and contribute effectively.

1.2 Application

This Code applies to all members and officers of the Society and, where applicable, to any person participating in a Society activity, project, competition, or other undertaking.

It applies to conduct occurring in connection with the Society, including at Society meetings, events, projects, competitions, programmes, external engagements, and on Society accounts, platforms, repositories, or other digital resources.

1.3 Relationship with College Rules and Applicable Law

This Code does not replace or supersede the rules of Maliyadeva College or applicable law. Members remain subject to College requirements at all times, and nothing in this Code limits the authority or disciplinary jurisdiction of the College.

Where a matter is governed by College requirements, the applicable College rules and procedures shall prevail.

1.4 Governing Principles

Members and officers shall conduct themselves in accordance with the following principles:

  1. Respect and dignity. Treat others with respect and dignity.

  2. Integrity and honesty. Act honestly and maintain academic and technical integrity.

  3. Responsibility. Fulfil commitments, take responsibility for one’s conduct, and use reasonable care in Society activities.

  4. Fairness. Treat others fairly and without unreasonable discrimination.

  5. Safety. Act with reasonable care for the safety and well-being of oneself and others.

  6. Responsible use of resources. Use Society property, funds, information, and digital resources responsibly and for their intended purposes.

  7. Accountability. Comply with applicable Society procedures, decisions, and College requirements.

Section 2. Expected Conduct

Members and officers are expected to conduct themselves responsibly in all matters connected with the Society. In particular, they shall:

2.1 Respectful Conduct

Treat other members, officers, teachers, guests, partners, and participants with respect and dignity, and communicate in a constructive and professional manner.

2.2 Responsible Participation

Participate responsibly in Society activities, projects, competitions, and meetings; fulfil commitments they undertake; follow applicable instructions and procedures; comply with approved project or competition requirements and applicable technical standards; obtain approval for material changes where required; and contribute to a cooperative working environment.

2.3 Safety and Proper Use of Facilities

Comply with applicable safety requirements and use College premises, Society facilities, equipment, and other resources with reasonable care and for their intended purposes.

2.4 Responsible Digital Conduct

Use Society accounts, repositories, communication platforms, shared documents, and other digital resources responsibly; protect credentials and access; and promptly report security concerns, suspected breaches, or loss of access to the appropriate officer.

2.5 Academic and Technical Integrity

Carry out Society work honestly and responsibly; accurately represent data, results, code, sources, and contributions; submit work that the member can explain and defend; and disclose the use of AI tools where required by the Society, a project, or a competition.

2.6 Responsible Representation

Members authorised to represent the Society shall communicate accurately, respectfully, and within the scope of their authority, and shall report relevant external communications as required by the Bylaws.

Section 3. Prohibited Conduct

The conduct described in this Section is prohibited. A breach may be addressed under the disciplinary procedures of the Bylaws and, where applicable, referred to the College.

3.1 Conduct Reserved to the College

Conduct that is subject to disciplinary regulation by the College shall remain governed by the applicable College requirements. The Society shall not establish, apply, or maintain a separate or inconsistent standard for matters reserved to the College.

Where conduct falling within the College’s disciplinary authority comes to the attention of the Executive Committee, the matter shall be referred to the appropriate College authority in accordance with the Bylaws, and the Society shall cooperate with any College inquiry or procedure.

Nothing in this Section limits the authority of the Society to take action within its own authority under the Bylaws, or to take reasonable interim protective measures to protect members, Society property, or the proper conduct of Society activities pending the outcome of a College procedure. Such interim measures shall not constitute a disciplinary sanction unless authorised as such under the Bylaws.

3.2 Dishonesty and Misconduct

Members shall not:

  1. engage in dishonesty, fraud, or deception in any Society matter;

  2. present the work of another person as their own, or present AI-generated work as their own without disclosure as required by Section 2.5;

  3. fabricate, falsify, or misrepresent data, results, code, or documentation; or

  4. engage in academic, technical, or competition misconduct.

3.3 Misuse of Society Property, Funds, or Resources

Members shall not:

  1. use Society funds, property, or resources for personal benefit or private purposes;

  2. remove, transfer, lend, dispose of, or damage Society property without authorization;

  3. misuse the Society’s name, logo, identity, or accounts; or

  4. commit, conceal, or facilitate any financial irregularity prohibited by the Bylaws or the Financial Regulations.

3.4 Unauthorised Representation and Misuse of Authority

Members shall not:

  1. represent the Society externally, or hold themselves out as acting for the Society, without authorization;

  2. make statements or commitments on behalf of the Society that have not been authorised;

  3. exercise authority delegated to them beyond the scope and limits of that delegation; or

  4. purport to bind the Society, the College, or any affiliated body without authority.

3.5 Misuse of Confidential Information, Data, or Digital Resources

Members shall not:

  1. access, use, disclose, or retain confidential information, personal data, or Society records without authorization;

  2. share credentials, access tokens, or account access with any person not authorized to hold them;

  3. alter, destroy, conceal, or remove Society data, records, or digital assets without authorization; or

  4. use Society digital resources in a manner that risks the security, integrity, or continuity of the Society’s systems.

3.6 Serious Disruption

Members shall not seriously disrupt, obstruct, or interfere with Society activities, meetings, projects, or procedures.

3.7 Interference with Complaints and Investigations

Members shall not interfere with, obstruct, or improperly influence a complaint, investigation, or disciplinary proceeding under the Bylaws.

3.8 General Standard

A member shall not intentionally or recklessly engage in conduct that, in a serious and material manner, undermines the purposes, safety, integrity, or lawful operation of the Society where the conduct is not otherwise addressed by this Section. Ordinary disagreement, good-faith criticism, or inadvertent error shall not, by itself, constitute a breach of this provision.

Section 4. Reporting and Cooperation

4.1 Reporting Concerns

Concerns regarding alleged violations of this Code of Conduct shall be reported in accordance with the disciplinary and complaint procedures established by Article X of the Bylaws.

4.2 Good Faith

Concerns should be raised in good faith. A concern that is later found not to be established does not, by itself, constitute a breach of this Code.

4.3 Cooperation

Members shall cooperate honestly with any legitimate inquiry, complaint, investigation, or disciplinary proceeding under the Bylaws.

4.4 No Retaliation

No person shall retaliate against a person who raises a concern, provides information, participates in an investigation, or otherwise exercises a right under this Code or the Bylaws.

4.5 Confidentiality

Information relating to a concern, complaint, or investigation shall be treated confidentially to the extent reasonably practicable and consistent with the Bylaws, College requirements, and applicable law.

Section 5. Enforcement and Interpretation

Breaches of this Code shall be dealt with in accordance with the disciplinary procedures established by the Bylaws.

Nothing in this Code limits the authority or disciplinary jurisdiction of Maliyadeva College or the application of applicable law.

Where any provision of this Code conflicts with the Constitution, the Bylaws, College requirements, or applicable law, the higher-ranking instrument shall prevail, and the conflicting provision shall be void to the extent of the conflict.
