Governing documents

The Society operates under five governing documents. The Constitution sets out what the Society is and who governs it; the others set out how it operates day to day.

  • Constitution The Society's foundational document — purpose, membership categories, governing bodies, amendment procedures.
  • Bylaws Operational detail — how meetings run, what each officer does, financial thresholds, disciplinary procedures.
  • Financial Regulations Rules on how Society funds are held, spent, authorised, and audited.
  • Membership Regulations Procedures for admission, progression from Associate to Regular Membership, and cessation.
  • Code of Conduct Standards of behaviour expected of all members and officers.
